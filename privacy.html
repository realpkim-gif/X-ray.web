Radiant

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Radiant is a research prototype that checks dog and cat X-rays for possible fractures. It has no server of its own: everything runs in your web browser. This page explains what information Radiant uses and where it goes.

Information from your Google account

When you sign in with Google, Radiant receives your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID. These are saved only in your browser, so Radiant can show who is signed in and keep your scans separate from anyone else's on the same computer. They are not sent to Radiant or to anyone else.

Your X-rays and results

  • Analysis happens on your device. The fracture model runs inside your browser. Your X-rays are never uploaded to Radiant for analysis.
  • Saved in your browser. Each X-ray you add is saved in your browser's storage, along with a thumbnail, its result and the heatmap and box overlays, so you can open it again under Previous scans.
  • Backed up to your own Google Drive. If you allow Drive access, Radiant copies those same scans to a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, so they appear on any device where you sign in. Only Radiant can use that folder. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

How Radiant uses Google user data

Radiant asks Google only for basic sign-in information (name, email, profile photo) and permission to use its own hidden app folder in your Drive (drive.appdata). This data is used only to sign you in and to save and sync your own scans. Radiant does not sell it, share it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Radiant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services your browser contacts

To load the site, your browser downloads files from GitHub Pages (which hosts Radiant), Google Fonts, and the jsDelivr and unpkg code libraries. Like any website, these services can see your IP address and browser details when they send those files. Radiant has no analytics, ads or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

  • One scan: open it under Previous scans and delete it. This removes it from your browser and from your Drive backup.
  • Sign out: removes your account details and Drive permission from this browser.
  • Everything: clear this site's data in your browser settings. To remove the Drive backup, go to Google Drive settings → Manage apps, find Radiant (shown as Radient) and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove Radiant's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Not a medical service

Radiant is an AI-assisted research prototype. It is not a veterinary diagnostic tool and does not replace evaluation by a qualified veterinarian.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. See also the Terms of Service. Questions? Open an issue on Radiant's GitHub page.
