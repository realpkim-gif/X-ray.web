02 · Principles

Why we built Radiant

Dogs can't say where it hurts, so radiographs do much of the explaining. Radiant explores how an AI model can point a reviewer toward areas worth a closer look while staying open about its uncertainty.

Meet the foundersMeet the founders→
01
Highlights, not diagnoses

Every result is framed as an area for additional review. Radiant never names a condition.

02
Uncertainty is always shown

Model confidence appears next to every highlight, with a note on what it does and doesn't mean.

03
Veterinarians make the call

Radiant supports your review. Clinical decisions belong to qualified professionals.
