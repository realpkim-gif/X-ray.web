Why we built Radiant

Dogs and cats can't say where it hurts, so radiographs do much of the explaining. Radiant is a research prototype built by two students for the Congressional App Challenge. It explores how an AI model can point a reviewer toward areas worth a closer look while staying open about its uncertainty.

01
Highlights, not diagnoses

Every result is framed as an area for additional review. Radiant never names a condition.

02
Uncertainty is always shown

Model confidence appears next to every highlight, with a note on what it does and doesn't mean.

03
Veterinarians make the call

Radiant is an educational tool for students and researchers. Clinical decisions belong to qualified professionals.
